What is the best body doubling app if I dislike video calls or webcams?

Stillalong recreates the quiet presence of studying with someone in the room without live cameras, microphones, or scheduling strangers. You open the pixel-art bedroom, sit across the desk from Hana and Mochi, and focus at your own pace.

Does Stillalong help with ADHD task initiation and late-night studying?

Yes. Starting is often the hardest part of studying alone. Stillalong keeps the environment predictable, quiet, and cozy—with warm night-sky lighting, desk lamp ambience, gentle soundscapes, and zero noisy leaderboards or streak guilt.

What makes Stillalong different from multiplayer study rooms like Discord or Focusmate?

Multiplayer platforms can introduce chat distractions, scheduling overhead, or camera self-consciousness. Stillalong is intentionally private and local-first, keeping all study history in your browser and charging $6.99 USD once rather than a monthly fee.

Can I track which subjects I studied after a body doubling session?

Yes. Before starting a focus block, you can tag your session (such as Math, Coding, Reading, Writing, or custom subjects) and view daily hours plus a Top Subjects breakdown inside the built-in Study Notebook.
